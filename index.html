My Why

Learning Has Always Been the Common Thread

As an Executive Chef, I discovered that my greatest satisfaction didn't come from creating exceptional meals—it came from mentoring people, building strong teams, solving problems, and helping others achieve more than they believed possible.

That experience shaped the way I approach learning today. Whether I'm leading a technology initiative, managing a development project, consulting with stakeholders, or designing a digital learning experience, I start with people and the outcomes we want to achieve.

Today, I combine leadership, instructional design, project management, accessibility, technology, and innovation to create learning experiences that empower students, faculty, teams, and organizations to grow.
