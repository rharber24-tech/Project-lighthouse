My Why

Learning Has Always Been the Common Thread

As an Executive Chef, I discovered that my greatest satisfaction didn't come from creating exceptional meals—it came from mentoring people, building strong teams, and helping others achieve more than they believed possible.

That passion eventually led me into instructional design and learning technology, where I found a new way to make an even greater impact.

Today, I combine leadership, instructional design, accessibility, technology, and innovation to create learning experiences that empower students, faculty, and organizations to grow.
