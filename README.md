# Project Lighthouse

## Rick Harber — Learning Leadership, Instructional Design & Learning Technology

Project Lighthouse is the professional portfolio of Rick Harber, showcasing leadership in instructional design, learning technology, AI innovation, project management, and digital learning strategy.

The portfolio demonstrates how learning initiatives can move from strategy through execution—connecting people, process, technology, and instructional design to create engaging, accessible, and scalable learning experiences.

## Featured Work

### Moodle Learning Platform Modernization
Leading instructional design strategy, project coordination, testing, standardization, and faculty enablement during a major Moodle modernization effort.

### AI-Powered Assessment Innovation
Exploring how AI can improve assessment workflows while preserving instructional quality, meaningful feedback, human judgment, and the learner experience.

### Interactive Recipe Book Experiences
Defining more than 500 culinary techniques and skills and leading the project from content development and filming through interactive learning design and Moodle implementation.

### Faculty & Learning Support Ecosystem
Designing scalable resources, workflows, and support experiences that help faculty and learning teams create consistent, accessible, and engaging digital learning experiences.

## Design Philosophy

Project Lighthouse is designed around six core values:

**Leadership**  
Connecting strategy, people, and execution.

**Innovation**  
Exploring technology thoughtfully and purposefully.

**Accessibility**  
Creating learning experiences that are usable and inclusive.

**Collaboration**  
Bringing instructional design, technology, content, and stakeholders together.

**Strategy**  
Designing learning initiatives around meaningful organizational and learner needs.

**Lifelong Learning**  
Continuously improving people, processes, systems, and experiences.
