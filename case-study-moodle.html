The Challenge

Modernizing the learning environment without losing sight of the learner.

Learning technology modernization is rarely just a technology project. Changes to an LMS affect instructors, instructional designers, learners, support teams, and the systems surrounding the learning experience.

The modernization effort required a balance between improving the institution's digital learning infrastructure, supporting faculty adoption, and maintaining a consistent learner experience.

My role required working across instructional design, faculty support, learning technology, and operations to help translate platform changes into practical learning solutions.

Strategic Approach

Connecting technology, people, and learning.

The work focused on more than implementing technology. It required creating the resources, processes, and support structures necessary for successful adoption.

Impact

Building a more scalable digital learning environment.

The value of LMS modernization extends beyond the platform itself. Successful implementation depends on whether people can understand, adopt, and use the technology effectively.

34+ Major instructional design and learning technology projects delivered.

4+ Learning platforms and digital learning ecosystems supported.
Leadership Takeaway

Technology succeeds when people can use it to create better learning.

This work reinforced a central principle of my leadership approach: learning technology should enable people, not become the focus of the experience. Effective modernization connects technology, instructional design, faculty enablement, accessibility, and learner experience.
