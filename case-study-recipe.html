The Challenge

Turning hundreds of culinary skills into a scalable learning experience.

Culinary education depends on more than recipes. Learners need to understand techniques, skills, ingredients, processes, sequencing, and execution—and they need to see how those pieces connect.

The project required defining and organizing more than 500 culinary techniques and skills into a structure that could support digital learning rather than simply reproduce traditional culinary resources.

The challenge extended beyond content design. The work required coordinating content definition, filming, multimedia production, interactive development, and eventual implementation within the Moodle learning environment.

Project Scope & Leadership
