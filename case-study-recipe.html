The Challenge

Turning static culinary resources into meaningful learning experiences.

Traditional recipe resources can provide valuable information, but information alone does not necessarily create an effective learning experience.

Culinary learners need to understand ingredients, techniques, processes, sequencing, and execution. The learning resource therefore needed to do more than present a recipe.

The opportunity was to transform recipe content into a digital experience that could guide learners through information while supporting engagement, usability, accessibility, and practical application.

Strategic Approach

Designing the recipe as a learning experience.

The design focused on organizing culinary content around the learner rather than simply reproducing the structure of a traditional recipe document.

Structure the Experience

Organized information into purposeful sections so learners could move through the content without being overwhelmed by large blocks of information.

Build Interaction

Used interactive learning elements to encourage exploration and create opportunities for learners to engage with content rather than simply read it.

Integrate Multimedia

Combined instructional text with visual and multimedia resources to support culinary concepts and make complex processes easier to understand.

Design for Accessibility

Applied accessibility and learner-centered design principles to create a resource that could support a broader range of learners.

Leadership Takeaway

Good learning design makes expertise usable.

The strongest digital learning experiences do not simply move existing content onto a screen. They reconsider how people interact with information, what they need to do with it, and how technology can make that experience more meaningful.
